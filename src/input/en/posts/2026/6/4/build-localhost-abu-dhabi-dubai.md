I'm happy to share that **Build //localhost** is coming to the UAE with two in-person community events in **Abu Dhabi** and **Dubai**. It is a great occasion to catch up on the latest Microsoft Build announcements, explore **Microsoft Foundry** and **GitHub Copilot**, see practical demos, and connect with the local
developer community.

The first stop is **Abu Dhabi**, on **Saturday June 6, 2026**, from **11:00 AM to 4:00 PM GST**, at **Bespin Global HQ, Al Wafra Square Offices, Al Reem Island**. I will attend as a participant and staff support, and I am looking forward to meeting friends from the community there. You can find more details and register on <a href="https://www.meetup.com/globalaiabudhabi/events/314932724/" target="_blank">Meetup</a> or on the <a href="https://globalai.community/events/build-localhost/37mubq8r/" target="_blank">Global AI Community event page</a>.

The second stop is **Dubai**, on **Tuesday June 9, 2026**, in person at **Dtec Auditorium, A5 Building, Dubai Digital Park, DSO**, from **3:00 PM to 7:00 PM GST**. I will be speaking there, together with community experts including <a href="https://www.linkedin.com/in/mahmoudatallah/" target="_blank">Mahmoud Atallah</a>, <a href="https://www.linkedin.com/in/ericmoneyang/" target="_blank">Eric Moneyang</a>, <a href="https://www.linkedin.com/in/hatimnagarwala/" target="_blank">Hatim Nagarwala</a>, and <a href="https://www.linkedin.com/in/ahmad-uzair/" target="_blank">Ahmad Uzair</a>. Please check the <a href="https://developer.microsoft.com/en-us/reactor/events/27261/" target="_blank">official Microsoft Reactor page</a> for the final agenda and timing.

If you are in the UAE and you work with Microsoft technologies, AI, developer tools, or you are simply curious to learn more, I hope to see you there!
