Busy weeks ahead in terms of events! I am pleased to announce that I will be a speaker, along with my colleague **<a href="https://www.linkedin.com/in/morgana-lalli-b5ab0a172/" target="_blank">Morgana Lalli</a>** (R&D Data Scientist, at Deltatre), in the second edition of **<a href="https://aiheroes.it/2024/" target="_blank">AI Heroes</a>** on **December 11th, 2024**, in presence in **Turin** and *online*.

*AI Heroes* is a full-day conference organized by <a href="https://synesthesia.it/" target="_blank">Synesthesia</a> with the support of numerous sponsors. The event offers a unique learning and networking opportunity for the community of developers, engineers, designers, and marketers working with artificial intelligence. Over 350 experts and enthusiasts are expected.

Check out the <a href="https://aiheroes.it/2024/agenda/" target="_blank">agenda</a> and join us at 12:50 PM, we will have the session **RAG-based AI Assistants: from demo to product, addressing real-world challenges**</a>: *we will share our journey to bring an enterprise AI-based assistant into production, addressing the real-world complexities of development and deployment, sharing our experience with security, advanced ETL integrations, automation, and frontend/backend customizations. From a prototype based on Azure OpenAI Chat Playground (aka Bring-Your-Own-Data), we’ll walk you through how we moved to a custom architecture, optimized to meet specific user and business needs, using Microsoft technologies, including Azure AI Search, Semantic Kernel, and Kernel Memory.*

To participate in the event, you can <a href="https://ticket.synesthesia.it/events/aih-24/" target="_blank">purchase tickets here</a>, while for more information, please visit the <a href="https://aiheroes.it/2024/" target="_blank">official website</a>.
