Also this year, I am pleased to invite you to the **<a href="https://www.wpc.education/?mtm_campaign=WPC_2024&mtm_kwd=Speaker&mtm_source=Social_MVP&mtm_content=giannirosagallina" target="_blank">WPC 2024</a>**, the main Italian conference on *Microsoft* technologies. Organized in Milan (Italy) **from November 26th to November 28th** by **OverNet Education**. The <a href="https://www.wpc.education/agenda-wpc?mtm_campaign=WPC_2024&mtm_kwd=Speaker&mtm_source=Social_MVP&mtm_content=giannirosagallina" target="_blank">list of speakers and sessions</a> is ready and available online: as usual, also this year it is very rich, featuring numerous sessions on artificial intelligence, web development, app development, the latest updates on development tools, and many many other topics!

Together with my dear friend **[Clemente Giorio](https://www.linkedin.com/in/clemente-giorio-03a61811/)** (Microsoft MVP, Intel Innovator), I will be speaker in the *AI Development* track in the afternoon, with the session **Generative AI + Spatial Computing: technologies and applications from the future** (presented in Italian). In the session we will see the latest developments in Deep Learning in this multidisciplinary field and explore techniques that allow us to reconstruct and analyze 3D scenes, objects, and people, discovering that uses and services once belonging to science fiction are now finally within our reach.

If you haven't <a href="https://www.wpc.education/ticket?mtm_campaign=WPC_2024&mtm_kwd=Speaker&mtm_source=Social_MVP&mtm_content=giannirosagallina" target="_blank">registered</a> yet, now is the time to do so!
