# Child Safety Standards Policy

This app is not a social publishing platform and does not allow user-generated content. None of the app's features enables or encourages users to share content or interact with other users. Nonetheless, in compliance with Google Play's Child Safety Standards Policy, I am providing the following information.

## 1. Introduction and Commitment

**Gianni's Hub** is committed to providing a safe and trusted online environment for all users, especially minors. I adhere to the Google Play Developer Policy Center requirements, the **Families Policy**, the **Child Safety Standards Policy**, and all applicable child protection laws and regulations, including but not limited to the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

The policies explicitly prohibit all forms of **Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE)**.

## 2. Prohibition of Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE)

I maintain a **zero-tolerance policy** against any content or behavior that sexually exploits, abuses, or endangers children. This includes, but is not limited to:

* **Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM):** Any visual depiction (photo, video, computer-generated image) of a minor engaging in sexually explicit conduct.
* **Grooming:** Engaging in behavior that attempts to establish a connection with a child for the purpose of sexual exploitation or abuse.
* **Child Trafficking or Sex-tortion.**

### **2.1. Content and Conduct Standards**

Being this app a non-social platform and not allowing user-generated content, there is no user-generated content or interaction. In any case, the following activities are strictly prohibited:

* Sharing, distributing, or attempting to distribute CSAM.
* Sexualizing or exploiting children in any way.
* Posting content that promotes self-harm, excessive violence, or dangerous activities.
* Harassment, bullying, or hate speech directed at minors.

## 3. Mandatory Safety Mechanisms

### **3.1. In-App User Feedback/Reporting Mechanism** 🚨

App users can report content or behavior that violates these standards by sending an e-mail to [giannishub@hotmail.com](mailto:giannishub@hotmail.com).

Reports are reviewed within 72 hours and appropriate action is taken swiftly, including content removal, and reporting to legal authorities.

### **3.2. Addressing Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM)**

Upon obtaining **actual knowledge** of CSAM content within the app (e.g., via user reports, or law enforcement notification), I will take **immediate and appropriate action** to immediately and permanently remove it from the app.

## 4. Compliance with Child Safety Laws and Regulations

### **4.1. Age Assurance and Audience Declaration**

* **Target Audience:** This app is intended for a general audience.
* **Age Gating:** There is no age gating implemented as the app does not collect personal information or allow user-generated content.

### **4.2. Data Privacy (COPPA/GDPR Compliance)**

* The **[Privacy Policy](./android_privacy.html)** explicitly details how the app handles user data.
* For apps targeting children or where children's data is processed: the app does not knowingly collect, use, or disclose personal information for any user, regardless of age.

## 5. Designated Child Safety Point of Contact

Being the author of the app, I am the point of contact to receive and manage notifications from Google Play or other authorities regarding CSAE content or child safety matters. To report any concerns, please send an email to [giannishub@hotmail.com](mailto:giannishub@hotmail.com).

## 6. Review and Updates

I am committed to the continuous review and improvement of these safety standards. This policy will be reviewed periodically or as required by new Google Play policies or changes in applicable laws.
